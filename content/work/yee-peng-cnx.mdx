## Where it came from

I lived in Chiang Mai for about three years. So much happened there, and those memories are some of the most precious I have. They are hard to forget, and I didn't want to keep them to myself.

When a chance came to build a project with a teammate, I knew what I wanted to make.

## What I wanted you to feel

I wanted to share the experience, not just the facts. Whoever opens the site should get a little of what Chiang Mai and its Yi Peng festival meant to me, even if they have never been there.

## How it plays

A 3D flip-book walks through the history of Yi Peng. A cultural timeline sits beside it. Three rituals can be tried in the browser: lanterns, Phang Pratheep and krathong. The site works in Thai and English, with Canvas effects.

## My role

I was the Experience Designer on a team of 2, covering UX/UI design and the cinematic look. The lead developer built it. I used generative AI tools along the way.
