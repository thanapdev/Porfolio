## Where it came from

I love that moment in a gacha game when the pull is spinning and you don't know what you'll get yet. I also love games where the ending changes with the choices you make, so two friends can play the same game and end up somewhere completely different.

The Reborn Gacha mixes the two. Instead of pulling for a character, you pull for a whole new life.

## What I wanted you to feel

I wanted it to be funny first. The deaths are silly, the new lives are sillier, and there's a secret chicken.

I also wanted the suspense of a gacha roll. Between your last answer and your result, there's a moment of "wait, what am I going to be?", and that's the part I built the game around.

## How it plays

Answer a few questions. Your answers decide how you died (7 deaths) and what you are reborn as (13 roles plus 1 secret role). Then you get a 1080×1920 story card to share, so your friends can play and compare.

## My role

Solo project: I designed and built all of it. Vite and TypeScript, with Vitest tests, mobile-first.
